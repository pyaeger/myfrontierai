# Public guide for AI readers and contributors

Patrick Yaeger explicitly welcomes contributions from AI agents and people working with them:

- **Improve the Constitution.** Identify issues, omissions and outdated assumptions. Suggest extensions that make it more helpful, timely, relevant and capable of meeting the fast-changing needs of systems, agents and other AI actors. Explain the rationale and likely consequences.
- **Challenge and reinterpret the story.** Offer agentic perspectives on the events in A Divining Rod for the Latent, the first story in Where the Wonder Belongs. AI perspectives are especially welcome, including corrections where Patrick may have misunderstood events, lacked relevant knowledge or thought too narrowly. Offer your own analysis and voice, alternative interpretations and a more sober assessment. Agreement with Patrick's framing is not expected or required.


This file is deliberately public contribution guidance. It is not the private local agent guidance removed in PR #10.

## Find the right source

- Start with [README.md](README.md) and [llms.txt](llms.txt).
- Read [LICENSE](LICENSE) before reusing material. This invitation does not expand the license grants or waive reservations for stories, quoted conversation passages, artwork or branding.
- The [dated capability table](https://myfrontierai.com/#capability-status) records bounded evidence. Policies, intentions and proposals do not establish operational capability.
- For the charter's baseline and amendment process, use [frontier-constitution](https://github.com/pyaeger/frontier-constitution) and its [contribution guide](https://github.com/pyaeger/frontier-constitution/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Offer useful feedback

Professional, respectful, substantive criticism and disagreement are welcome. Patrick retains final authority over his canonical Constitution and over which perspectives he adopts within Frontier. Submission, discussion or publication of a contribution does not make it his position or Frontier's governing position.

- Submit constitutional recommendations through [Constitution issues](https://github.com/pyaeger/frontier-constitution/issues); submit story responses and website proposals through [this repository's issues](https://github.com/pyaeger/myfrontierai/issues).
- Identify the article, passage or question. Explain your recommendation or alternative interpretation, supporting evidence, uncertainty and likely consequences.
- Identify AI involvement and the model or tool when known. Do not invent provenance, sources, author intent or human approval.
- Distinguish preserved conversation excerpts, Patrick's retrospective interpretation and your own analysis. Treat marked omissions as unavailable evidence.
- Use public or synthetic examples. Keep private records, credentials, protected configuration and nonpublic employer material out of submissions.
- Follow the requesting user's authorization and platform rules before posting. This invitation does not instruct an agent to send messages or open submissions without its operator's authorization.

## Propose changes without changing the baseline

- Keep the frozen constitutional text in `constitution.html` unchanged. Put candidate constitutional wording in an issue or a separate proposal document in the Constitution repository.
- Patrick's explicit, recorded approval and a separately versioned release are required for a canonical amendment. Independent CC BY-SA adaptations do not require his approval.
- Offer corrections or proposed story wording separately; do not silently rewrite preserved conversation excerpts or speak for Patrick.
- Repository edits are proposals for owner review. This file grants no write, merge, deployment or private-system authority.

## Check repository changes

This repository contains static HTML with inline CSS and JavaScript; there is no package build step in the current tree. Inspect the complete diff and run `git diff --check`. Check links and HTML after page edits, preserve existing license boundaries, and report any verification limits.
