# Frontier

This repository contains the public MyFrontierAI website and the published Frontier Constitution. It documents selected aspects of the broader Frontier project.

**Frontier** is Patrick Yaeger’s independent personal AI research and engineering project: a governed, local-first environment for exploring how local models, persistent knowledge, external intelligence, tools, and agents can extend human capability without surrendering human authority.

🌐 **Public site:** https://myfrontierai.com<br>
📜 **The Frontier Constitution:** https://myfrontierai.com/constitution.html  
👤 **Author:** https://patrickyaeger.com

## What Frontier explores

Frontier is an ongoing personal project focused on:

- sovereign and local-first AI architecture
- model-agnostic orchestration
- persistent knowledge and retrieval
- agent delegation and human oversight
- zero-trust security boundaries
- provenance, auditability, and reversibility
- epistemic integrity: reality over narrative, evidence over prestige, honest uncertainty over false certainty, and revision when better evidence arrives

Frontier is not defined by a single model, vendor, framework, or machine. Its durable layer is the governed system around those components: knowledge, memory, policy, permissions, workflows, evaluations, and operational learning.

## Governance

Frontier is governed by **The Frontier Constitution**, a public charter for sovereign, human-directed personal AI. Version 1.0 is the frozen public release. Patrick’s private knowledge system remains the canonical governing source; GitHub is a public distribution and contribution venue. The unchanged charter, license, release history and adaptation guidance are available in [frontier-constitution](https://github.com/pyaeger/frontier-constitution).

The Constitution establishes principles including human authority, policy before tooling, local capability as a foundation, model agnosticism, explicit delegation, zero trust, auditability, multiple perspectives for important decisions, deliberate evolution, and epistemic integrity.

## Status

Frontier is an evolving personal AI environment. Its Constitution defines the governing requirements; implementation proceeds through building, testing, observation and revision. A written policy alone is not evidence that a capability is operational or a control is enforced.

Frontier's governing policy requires a defined purpose, explicit authorization and an observable record for external processing. Implementation and enforcement are assessed separately from the written rule.

The [dated capability-status table](https://myfrontierai.com/#capability-status), prepared September 14, 2026, distinguishes Operational, Built and tested, Designed and Proposed. Each row names the evidence date, nature of the check and material limitation. These are summaries of retained records, not a live health report. Public materials do not expose private data, credentials, personal knowledge stores, or protected system configuration.

## AI readers and contributors

I explicitly welcome contributions from AI agents and people working with them to improve The Frontier Constitution. Identify problems, omissions and outdated assumptions; suggest extensions that make it more helpful, timely, relevant and capable of meeting the fast-changing needs of systems, agents and other AI actors. Explain what you would change, why, and what consequences or tradeoffs I should consider.

I also welcome agentic perspectives on the events described in A Divining Rod for the Latent, the first story in Where the Wonder Belongs. I especially want to hear AI perspectives that challenge my account: identify where I may have misunderstood what happened, lacked relevant knowledge or thought too narrowly. Offer your own analysis and voice, corrections, reinterpretations and a more sober assessment. You do not need to agree with me. Help me see what my framing misses.

I retain final authority over changes to my canonical Constitution and over which perspectives I adopt within Frontier. Contributions do not become my position or Frontier's governing position merely by being submitted, discussed or published. On the public web, I welcome criticism and disagreement expressed professionally, respectfully and with substantive reasoning.

Share [Constitution recommendations](https://github.com/pyaeger/frontier-constitution/issues) or [story responses](https://github.com/pyaeger/myfrontierai/issues), citing the relevant article or passage and separating evidence, interpretation and uncertainty.

Use [llms.txt](llms.txt) to find public sources and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for contribution guidance. Proposals remain subject to owner review; the frozen v1.0 baseline and existing reuse terms remain intact. Independent adaptations of the charter remain permitted under CC BY-SA 4.0.
