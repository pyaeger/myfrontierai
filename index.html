The idea

MyFrontierAI is a personal AI operating system: your notes, your work, your thinking — transformed into an interconnected web of meaning your own AI can traverse at will. A second brain that doesn't just store, it connects, surfaces, and reasons over everything you know.

Sovereign by design. Your models, your data, your hardware. Real capability, with nothing leaving your control — the opposite of renting your mind back to someone else's cloud.

What I help you build

Second Brain (PARA + RAG)
