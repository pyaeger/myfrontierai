The idea

MyFrontierAI is a personal AI operating environment: notes, projects, models, policies, and tools connected so that intelligence can work across context without making any single vendor, model, or platform the permanent center.

Sovereign by design. Frontier's governing policy requires a defined purpose, explicit authorization and an observable record for external processing. Implementation and enforcement are assessed separately from the written rule.

What “sovereign AI” means here. User-controlled computing, data governance, model selection, delegation policy, and technological autonomy. It does not refer to political sovereignty, legal immunity, or affiliation with the sovereign-citizen movement.

What Frontier is designed to support

Frontier is an evolving personal AI environment. Its Constitution defines the governing requirements; implementation proceeds through building, testing, observation and revision. A written policy alone is not evidence that a capability is operational or a control is enforced.

Second Brain (PARA + RAG)

Knowledge organized and made searchable by meaning, with explicit boundaries for what may be indexed, synchronized, or delegated.

Panel of Experts

Multiple local and external models used as interchangeable specialists, with disagreement and uncertainty preserved rather than flattened.

Personal AI Assistant

A human-directed assistant that can work from durable personal context while remaining constrained by policy, permissions, and oversight.

Sovereign Architecture

A zero-trust foundation combining local capability, network isolation, controlled delegation, backup, recovery, and auditability.
